Our history

From one mixer to a regional supplier.

Singhawat Concrete began in 1994 with a single mixer truck and a yard outside Bangkok, supplying small residential pours for builders who needed concrete that arrived when it was promised.

Today the plant runs a full precast line alongside ready-mixed production, and the same family still signs off on every mix design that leaves the gate.

Vision & mission

What we are aiming at, and how we get there.

Vision

To be the concrete supplier that contractors in the region name first, trusted for consistency of mix, honesty about lead times, and a standard that does not move when the schedule gets tight.

Mission

To test every batch against its specification, to keep our fleet and precast beds running to a published schedule, and to price openly so a builder can plan a project around us without hedging.

Our policy

Standards we do not negotiate.

  • Every batch is cube-tested and the results are kept on file for the customer.
  • Mix designs are matched to the specified strength class, never substituted downward.
  • Delivery windows are quoted honestly, and we call ahead when a pour is at risk.
  • Plant, fleet, and precast beds are maintained on a documented schedule.
